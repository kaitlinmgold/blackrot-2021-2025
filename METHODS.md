# Analysis notes

- **Experimental unit:** plot, with recorded block within each trial year. Programs were randomized annually; program inventories changed among years. Class is an attribute of a complete program, not a balanced factorial treatment. The data do not establish independence of physical panels across years.
- **Incidence:** symptomatic clusters divided by valid rated clusters (19 or 20), modeled with a beta-binomial logit mixed model.
- **Severity:** plot mean percentage of diseased berries per cluster, including zeros. Models use `log((p + 0.5)/(100.5 - p))`, Gaussian errors, and year-plus-class residual dispersion. Severity is not a measure conditional on infection.
- **Primary inference:** fixed year and class; random block-within-year and program-year intercepts. Incidence class inference uses the regular likelihood-ratio test. Incidence interaction and severity omnibus inference use refitted null-model bootstrap calibration. Approximate Kenward–Roger summaries and naive likelihood-ratio diagnostics do not replace calibrated omnibus tests.
- **Pooled estimates:** additive-model marginal means with equal weight across observed years, and six Holm-adjusted class contrasts per endpoint. Annual means are descriptive. Nonsignificance does not establish equivalence or constant efficacy.
- **Secondary analyses:** metal comparisons concern whole programs in 2023–2025, not isolated metal effects. Within-year program-versus-control screens use program + block models on transformed percentages; HC3 and single-plot omission diagnostics characterize their limitations.
- **2022 assignment sensitivity:** eight records belonging to Y2022_P02/P03 have unresolved program/block assignment. They remain in primary class models, are omitted from program-specific screens and Figures 4–5, and are removed in the completed 256-plot sensitivity. See `analyses/sensitivity_2022/`.
- **Missing ratings:** one invalid negative and two missing cluster ratings are not imputed. Affected denominators are 19.
- **Figures 4–5:** percent control is `100 × (1 − program mean / same-year untreated mean)`, separately by endpoint. Figure 4 has 59 program-years; Figure 5 has 42 overlapping product memberships from 35 program-years. These are descriptive arithmetic means, not model-estimated class means or isolated product effects.
- **Figure 6:** modeled infection periods overlapping the first experimental spray through final rating, inclusive. Counts for 2021–2025 are 21, 10, 10, 14, 13. Trial windows differ; these are weather-model flags, not observed fruit infections. Bloom dates are unavailable, so calendar alignment is not phenological alignment.

Recorded primary tests: incidence class LR = 37.400879 (3 df, P < 0.001); incidence interaction bootstrap P = 0.3781774; severity class bootstrap P = 0.0001000; severity interaction bootstrap P = 0.0723242. Primary pooled estimates are in `results/primary/`; calibrated tests and simulation-level results are in `results/primary_bootstrap/`. Seeds, successful/failed refit counts, and failure bounds are recorded with each bootstrap result.
