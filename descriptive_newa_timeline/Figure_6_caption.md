Figure 6. Timing of modeled black rot infection periods, experimental fungicide applications, and final disease assessments in five annual Niagara grape trials, 2021–2025. Burgundy bars span grouped periods classified as black rot infection events in the NEWA Grape Infection Events Log for the Geneva (AgriTech Crittenden), NY weather station. Teal markers indicate experimental application dates, and dashed lines indicate final assessment dates. Gray shading identifies the period after assessment. Totals at right count each grouped infection period overlapping the first experimental application through the final assessment date, inclusive, once. Trial windows differed among years. Grouped periods may include interruptions in leaf wetness and indicate weather conducive to infection rather than confirmed infections of fruit. Calendar dates are aligned across years; actual Niagara bloom dates were unavailable. Historical model records were retrieved September 21, 2026.
