Figure 4. Percent control of final cluster black rot severity (A) and incidence (B) by fungicide-program class, 2021–2025. Each point represents one complete program evaluated in one year. Color identifies year and shape identifies application strategy. Percent control was calculated as 100 × (1 − program mean / same-year untreated mean), separately for each endpoint, using arithmetic means of four replicate plots. Boxes show the interquartile range, horizontal lines indicate medians, and whiskers span the full observed range. Biological-only, mixed, and conventional-only classes contain 34, 15, and 10 program-year observations, respectively. Biological-only follows the operational classification in the text, including metal-containing materials. Two unresolved 2022 program identities were excluded. These descriptive distributions give equal weight to each program-year and do not represent the model-estimated class means or their confidence intervals.

Figure 5. Percent control of final cluster black rot severity (A) and incidence (B) for programs containing selected recurring products, 2021–2025. Each point represents the outcome of a complete program, including its other materials and application sequence. Color identifies year and shape distinguishes single biological products, biological combinations or sequences, and programs that include conventional fungicides. Control was calculated relative to the same-year untreated mean as in Figure 4. A program may appear in more than one product group; 42 group memberships represent 35 unique program-year observations. Boxes show the interquartile range and median, with whiskers spanning the full observed range, for groups with at least five observations. Howler and Howler EVO are separate groups, and Ensendo is excluded from the Howler group. Regalia and Stargus occurred together. Asterisks identify OxiDate and PERpose groups that combine recorded name variants; formulation labels, rates, and application sequences are given in Supplementary Table S1.
